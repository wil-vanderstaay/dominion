About

Play all your boxes at once, without shuffling every randomizer together.

Tick what you own and you'll get how many cards to take from each box — the same spread as one big shuffle, so bigger expansions come up more often.

The buttons

🎲 Draw 10 — draws from everything ticked.

🎰 Random Mix — cuts your ticks down to a random few first. Set how many in ⚙.

Splits, not cards

Shuffle each box's own randomizers and take the number shown. That's your Kingdom.

Platinum & Colony

With Prosperity in, one of the ten cards is picked at random. If it came from Prosperity, play with Platinum and Colony.
